What NYTHOS tracks on Ethereum

Most whale trackers broadcast raw transaction sizes and call it a day. NYTHOS goes further — every Ethereum signal is scored, and verified after the fact so you can audit how reliable it actually is.

  • Large-wallet accumulation — coordinated ETH buys across multiple wallets in a short window
  • Dormant wallet reactivation — wallets silent for months or years suddenly moving funds
  • Smart money clusters — 3+ known profitable wallets entering the same position together
  • DEX and bridge flows — large capital movement across Uniswap, Curve, and major bridges
  • Exchange inflow / outflow spikes — unusual ETH deposits or withdrawals on major CEXes

How NYTHOS scores confidence

Every signal gets a HIGH, MED, or LOW confidence rating based on cluster size, timing patterns, historical wallet accuracy, and on-chain follow-through. HIGH-confidence signals have the strongest track record.

After 24 hours, the engine checks what actually happened and marks each signal as a verified hit or miss. Browse the public proof feed to judge accuracy yourself before trusting any signal.
