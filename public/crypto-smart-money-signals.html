How confidence scoring works

Every NYTHOS signal is rated before you see it. The engine weighs cluster size, timing patterns, wallet historical accuracy, and on-chain follow-through to assign one of three confidence tiers:

HIGH

Strong cluster size, tight timing, wallets with proven track records. Highest historical accuracy.

MED

Moderate cluster or mixed wallet histories. Worth tracking but treat with some caution.

LOW

Early or ambiguous signal. Could develop into something — useful for watchlist seeding.
